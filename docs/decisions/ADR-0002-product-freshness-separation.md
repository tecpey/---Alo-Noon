# ADR-0002: Separate Fresh Signature and Packaged Product Promises

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-23

## Decision

Only premium bakery-specific signature breads use the fresh made-to-order fulfillment model. Ordinary traditional breads and specialty breads are sold as packaged stock. Limited cakes and pastries are deferred to a later phase.

## Consequence

Catalog, pricing, packaging, quality controls, logistics and customer messaging must be driven by Product Class and Fulfillment Mode.
